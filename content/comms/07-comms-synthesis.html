Synthesis and the Mixed Bench

One conversation has been running through this module since it began — 3.4 kHz of speech on a telephone line. Here are all five ways of sending it on one pair of axes, followed by a set under exam conditions.

7.1 One conversation, five ways

Start at a 30 dB channel and step through the buttons. The left panel is the whole module in one shape.

  • The analog schemes are sloped lines. Whatever the channel gives, they hand you, plus or minus a constant: SSB exactly, AM 4.8 dB worse because two thirds of the power is in a carrier that says nothing, FM 14 dB better because it spent twelve times the bandwidth to get there.
  • The digital ones are flat. 49.9 dB, set by the quantiser, entirely independent of the channel — and then they stop. Not fade: stop.

That is not a difference of degree. An analog link gets steadily worse; a digital link is perfect and then absent. Everything Part 4 spent on bandwidth was buying that shape.

And the right panel settles the argument

At a 30 dB channel, 16-ary PCM sits at 2.35 times the message bandwidth and 49.9 dB, against broadcast FM's 12 times and 44.0 dB. That is five times narrower and six decibels better at the same time — not a trade, a win on both axes. So why did FM last a century? Because of the proviso. Shannon's 1 + S/N = M² demands a 24.1 dB channel before 16-ary is possible at all, and for most of the twentieth century the line between two cities was not that good. Drag the knob down below 24 dB and watch that scheme simply cease to exist while FM carries on. The bound from Part 5 is not an abstraction; it is the thing that chose the technology.

7.2 How few ideas were doing the work

Seven parts, and nearly all of it came from four sentences.

The ideaWhere it did the work
Multiplying by a carrier shifts a spectrum Part 1's frequency-shift property is the whole of Part 2, and the reason a modulated signal sits at ±fc with a sideband either side. Mixing, heterodyning and every superhet receiver are the same line read backwards.
Narrow in one domain is wide in the other Part 1's duality. It sets AM's 2fm, Carson's rule, the bandwidth a pulse needs, and — through Rb = nfs — the whole bill PCM presents in Part 4.
Bandwidth and power are exchangeable, at a rate FM's β² for 2fm(β+1) in Part 3; PCM's 6 dB per bit for n×B in Part 4; M-ary's log₂M saving for M²−1 of SNR in Part 5. Three parts, one trade, three different exchange rates.
Orthogonal signals do not interfere Part 1's transform pairs rest on it, QPSK's two carriers use it to double the rate for free in Part 5, and Part 6 shows it is the single mechanism behind FDM, TDM and CDMA.

7.3 The mistakes that cost the most

MistakeThe check that catches it
Using S/N in decibels inside Shannon's formulaConvert first. 30 dB is 1000, not 30 — worth a factor of two in the answer.
Dropping fm from Carson's rule2(Δf + fm), not 2Δf. The second term matters whenever β is not large.
Confusing the two Nyquist theoremsfs ≥ 2B governs the sampler; Rs ≤ 2B governs the channel. Different theorems, opposite ends of the chain.
Using M where log₂M belongsM levels carry log₂M bits. 16 points is 4 bits, not 16.
Forgetting Es/N₀ = k·Eb/N₀A symbol carrying k bits carries k times the energy. In decibels that is +10 log₁₀k, not ×k.
Quoting the 6.02n + 1.76 figure without its conditionIt is a full-scale number, and it falls a decibel per decibel as the signal drops. That is the entire argument for companding.
Baud where bit/s was askedThey are equal only for binary signalling — which is the case the question is usually trying to move you off.

7.5 A set under exam conditions

Everything from all seven parts, mixed, at exam pace.

This module is about one question asked six times: what does it cost to move a message, and what does the money buy? Part 1 supplied the machinery. Parts 2 and 3 spent power and bandwidth on an analog message and showed they are exchangeable at a rate. Part 4 stopped sending the message and sent numbers instead, at a factor of n in bandwidth. Part 5 found the wall that numbers run into. Part 6 shared the channel among many.

Only one of those is a law. Every trade in this module has an escape — more power, more bandwidth, a better receiver, a cleverer code — except Shannon's. That one has none, and everything else in communications engineering is manoeuvring in the space it leaves. The right answer to a channel that cannot carry what you need is never a better modulation; it is a different channel.

And the same conversation carried it all. 3.4 kHz of speech, sampled at 8 kHz, eight bits, 64 kbit/s, one DS0, twenty-four to a T1 — the numbers arrived in Signal Processing Part 2 and have appeared in every part since. The compilation's triangle came too: QPSK's four symbols sit on the unit circle at 45°, 16-QAM's minimum distance is 2/√10, and √10 is the hypotenuse of the 1–3 right triangle the 3-4-5 cast is drawn from. Nine knowledge areas in, the evidence is fairly strong that these are not seventeen subjects.
